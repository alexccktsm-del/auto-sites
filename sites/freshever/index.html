Who We Are

Rooted in freshness and powered by cold-chain care, FreshEver brings premium frozen goodness directly to KL homes and businesses.

Trusted quality • FreshEver

Our Story

A shop for frozen food. Located in KL. We handpick quality seafood, meats, produce, and ready-to-cook meals, locking in peak nutrition and flavor from farm or fishery to freezer. Whether you’re meal-prepping for the week or stocking a restaurant, we make freshness effortless—every day.

  • Farm-to-freezer sourcing for consistent taste
  • Strict temperature control throughout delivery
  • Curated selection tailored for KL households and F&B

Our Services

From everyday meals to event-scale orders, we’ve got you covered with convenient, quality-first options.

Frozen Produce & Proteins

Premium seafood, poultry, meats, and vegetables—flash-frozen to preserve peak texture, color, and nutrition.

Ready-to-Cook Meals

Chef-curated dumplings, marinated cuts, and oven-ready mains—great taste with minimal prep time.

Bulk & Wholesale

Consistent quality and pricing for cafes, caterers, and restaurants with scheduled supply and easy reorders.

Cold-Chain Delivery

Reliable KL-wide delivery in chilled vehicles, keeping your order at safe temperatures from our door to yours.
